2. Sequencing Hypotheses & Interference Control

kanji-first-stack (HS-T009 Turns 5-6)

Order: latin → cyrillic → arabic → kanji

Four-layer rotational exposure pairing distinct cognitive regimes with low visual overlap.

greek-first-protocol (HS-F001 / HS-T009 Turns 24-26)

Order: greek → cyrillic → hebrew → arabic → kana → kanji

Gradual progression starting from Greek alphabetic familiarity before introducing Cyrillic, Semitic abjads, kana, and logographic characters.

typological-distance (HS-F013 (100 Poems Roadmap))

Order: greek → cyrillic → hebrew → arabic → hangul → devanagari → kana → georgian → armenian → ethiopic → cherokee → tifinagh → kanji

Twelve-script sequence ordered by structural distance from Latin, with Kanji run as a slow parallel track.

Traces Psalm 33:6 and John 1:1 across Hebrew dabar, Greek logos, Latin verbum, and Chinese dao without collapsing them into a fake shared root.
