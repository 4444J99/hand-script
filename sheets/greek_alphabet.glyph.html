Methodological Note: Faint trace glyphs come from standard typographic fonts to indicate structural proportion and component placement, not a calligraphic master exemplar. Handwriting practice builds grapheme-motor recall and orthographic awareness; track reading comprehension separately.

Six-Step Notebook Session Protocol (15-20 min baseline):
1. warm up (15%): Write baseline strokes, curves, or radicals slowly with focus on proportion.
2. copying (25%): Copy a short canonical line or glyph sequence attentively onto physical paper.
3. memory rewrite (20%): Cover the exemplar and rewrite the target sequence from visual-motor recall.
4. observation (15%): Annotate recurring graphemes, diacritics, positional allographs, or roots.
5. composition (15%): Write one original variation, word recombination, or script adaptation.
6. reflection (10%): Record one concise note on friction, legibility, or sound-sign mapping.
